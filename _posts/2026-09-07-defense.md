On September 7 2026, I successfully defended my PhD Dissertation "Geometrically Consistent Shape Matching with Optimal Cycles in Product Graphs" and I am very honored to have passed it with highest distinction (summa cum laude).
Looking back on very exciting three years including:
- 14 conferences visits (CVPR New Orleans, GCPR Konstanz, CVPR Vancouver, SIGGRAPH LA, GCPR Heidelberg, CVPR Seattle, GCPR Munich, ECCV Milano, SGP Bilbao, CVPR Nashville, GCPR Freiburg, ICCV Hawaii, 3DV Vancouver, CVPR Denver)
- 7 first author papers (2 of which shared first authorships)
- 17 paper submitted/accepted (16 of which accepted on the first try, i.e. only one resubmission)
- 1 talk at the Lamarr Scientific Forum at Bonn
- 3 orals at conferences (at GCPR 2022, at CVPR at 2023, at GCPR 2024)
- 1 highlight paper (at ICCV 2025)
- 1 best student paper runner up (at CVPR 2023)
- countless sleepless nights during deadline phases
